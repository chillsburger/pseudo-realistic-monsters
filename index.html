01 / The feature

Growing up.
Getting haunted.

SCREENPLAY COMPLETE

Vinny is a former altar boy, a fledgling artist, and the supreme misfit in his suburban hell-hole.

Since childhood, he and his best friend Jade have been contacting a spirit through a homemade Ouija board. Chris becomes the confidant Vinny can’t find among the living: a gay punk ghost who slips into his dreams.

It’s 1992. Vinny wants out, wants art school, wants to finally hook up. But when he gets close to another boy, Chris turns jealous. Under the specter of AIDS and the weight of Catholic guilt, desire begins to feel like a threat. The ghost’s nightmares spill into waking life.

Pseudo-Realistic Monsters follows Vinny’s coming of age within his Italian American family and reaches into Chris’s past, where queer love, loss, and rage refuse to stay buried.

02 / The proof of concept

The Ghost at
Skeleton Rock

The short film opens a door into the world of the feature. After his first hookup, Vinny turns to Chris, the ghost he contacts through a handmade Ouija board. When he brings Gabe beneath a crumbling bridge to summon him, Chris proves real, jealous, and capable of twisting reality into nightmares.

The feature expands this world, exploring Chris’s backstory and Vinny’s life beyond the bridge.

03 / The filmmaker

Bobby Abate

Queer artist, filmmaker, and editor.

Bobby Abate’s work draws on memory, desire, and the uncanny. Their films have screened at the New York Film Festival, International Film Festival Rotterdam, and the Museum of Modern Art. They received a 2024 Princess Grace Special Projects Grant and edited seasons three and four of HBO’s We’re Here.
